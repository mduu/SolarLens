## Description

```
Solar Lens brings your Solar Manager system to iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and CarPlay. See at a glance what your roof produces, what your home uses and where the energy goes — and act on it right away, from the charging mode to an automation.

LIVE
• Energy flow between solar, battery, grid and home in real time
• Solar forecast for today and the next two days
• Pin the devices you care about to the app's main screen
• Day charts for production, consumption, grid and battery — for past days too

CONTROL
• Switch your EV charging mode, including target charge and departure time
• Change device priorities by drag and drop
• Set the battery mode (Standard, Eco, Peak shaving, Manual)

AUTOMATIONS AND ALERTS
• Battery to Car: charges the car from the house battery, down to a minimum level you choose
• Auto-reset Charging Mode: charge at full power now and switch back at a time you set — even when the app is closed
• Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while an automation runs
• Alerts when battery level, production, consumption, grid import, grid export or charging power crosses a value you set

STATISTICS
• Week, month, year, your whole installation or any custom range
• Flow: where your energy came from and where it went
• Balance: which days, months or years ran a surplus or a deficit
• Map: every hour, day or month tinted by autarky, self-consumption or balance
• Export as CSV or Excel

UNDERSTAND YOUR SYSTEM
• Autarky and self-consumption for today, 7, 30 and 365 days
• Grid costs and revenue based on your own tariffs
• Battery advantage: what your battery has saved you
• Battery simulator: replays a past year of your own data with extra battery capacity and shows whether it would pay off
• CO₂ avoided, calculated with your country's grid mix

EVERYWHERE
• Apple Watch app that works without the iPhone, with complications and Smart Stack widgets
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
• Siri and Shortcuts on iPhone and iPad: "How much solar am I producing?"
• CarPlay: energy overview, charging mode and device priorities
• English, German, French, Italian and Danish

PRIVATE
Solar Lens talks directly to Solar Manager. Your login, your devices and your measurements are never sent to the Solar Lens server.

For the living room there is Solar Lens BigScreen for Apple TV, a separate app.

Tip
On Apple Watch, the easiest way to enter your login is with your iPhone keyboard.

Requirements
You need a login to an existing Solar Manager installation.

Disclaimer
Solar Lens is an independent hobby project by Marc Dürst and is not affiliated with Solar Manager AG. It uses the official Solar Manager API.
```
