Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how 'Solar Lens' collects, uses, and protects your personal information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely.

Information We Collect

  • Login Credentials: We store your login credentials locally on your device using Apple's Keychain. This information is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Notification token (iOS only): When you use an automation with a scheduled end time, the app sends a push notification identifier (APNs token) to our server, together with the moment it should wake your device and the notification text your device prepared for it.
  • Background images (Apple TV only): When you send a custom background image to your Apple TV, that image is stored on our server for the time it takes to hand it over.

We collect no usage statistics, no location data and no advertising identifiers. We do no tracking and share nothing for advertising purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • Login Authentication: We use your stored login credentials to authenticate you when you log in to the app.
  • Scheduled automations: The notification token is used for one purpose only: waking your device at the agreed moment so the automation runs on time, even when the app is closed. The automation itself runs entirely on your device. Our server knows neither your Solar Manager credentials nor your devices or measurements, and never accesses the Solar Manager API itself.
  • Image transfer: The background image is only passed along until your Apple TV has collected it.

How Long We Keep It

  • A wake-up entry is deleted as soon as its notification has been sent, or once its period has expired.
  • A background image is deleted immediately after your Apple TV collects it, and at the latest after 24 hours.
  • If Apple tells us a device identifier is no longer valid, we delete every entry belonging to it.

Your Choice

You can turn server-assisted timing off at any time under Settings → Automations → "Server-assisted timing". Doing so deletes every wake-up entry stored for your device and stops new ones from being created. Automations keep working, but may run later.

Data Sharing

  • We do not share your personal information with any third parties, except as required by law or to provide essential services.
  • The notification token is passed to Apple (Apple Push Notification service), because notifications can only be delivered through Apple.
  • The app uses the Solar Manager API, which is owned and operated by Solar Manager AG. We may share your login credentials with Solar Manager AG to access their services, but we do not share any other personal information with them.

Data Security

  • We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
  • Your login credentials are stored securely in Apple's Keychain, which uses advanced encryption techniques to protect sensitive data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and correct your personal information in your Apple Keychain.
  • Deletion of your personal information in your Apple Keychain.
  • Object to the processing of your personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting a revised version on our website or in the app.
