### English
- On iPad, Solar Lens now opens to a dashboard that shows energy flow, today's chart, forecast, efficiency and your devices on a single screen.
- A sidebar takes you straight to every area on iPad, with keyboard shortcuts for sections, refresh and settings.
- Tap a tile on the iPad dashboard and its details open beside it, so the overview stays in view.
- The charging view now charts today's charging power and how much your car charged over the last seven days.
- On a large iPad, statistics shows Flow, Balance, Map and Bars side by side, and Today adds the energy flow next to the chart.
- When a battery or charger runs in Solar Manager's new Aria mode, Solar Lens now shows it correctly.
- Switching the statistics period no longer briefly shows the previous period's bars.
